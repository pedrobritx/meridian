## Explicit accessibility walkthrough

- [ ] Function available with all decorative effects disabled.
- [ ] Native semantic control state is exposed and accurate.
- [ ] Keyboard can perform the full task without a drag gesture.
- [ ] Visible focus remains stable through visual transition.
- [ ] Reduced motion yields immediate/quiet equivalent meaning.
- [ ] Forced colours and increased contrast preserve identification/action.
- [ ] Reduced transparency or unsupported backdrop effects use an opaque surface.
- [ ] Text/controls are readable over actual composited backgrounds.
- [ ] Focus returns appropriately on dismiss; no keyboard trap.
- [ ] Screen reader correctly identifies actionable controls and updates.
- [ ] Touch target size and proximity remain usable on the tested device.
- [ ] Interaction latency/frame behaviour is measured using a specified metric, not assumed.

Each checked item needs supporting test conditions and evidence or should remain unchecked.

### Publication gate

Publish only an anonymised aggregate or permission-appropriate summary. Keep identifying raw observations and consent records outside public GitHub. Do not equate visual preference with usability superiority or imply clinical benefits without an appropriate research design.
