What the system does here

    The four layers

    • LightThe environment's key light, or the real sun. Your pointer, finger, focus or gaze adds a local light.
    • GroundThe environment itself: colour, haze and a texture that follows its logic: Desert is granular, Prism refracts light, Ocean flows, Aurora drifts, Forest sways, Canyon is layered rock. Motion is very slow and stops with reduced motion; never under body text.
    • ContentCalm, solid, highest contrast. Never refracts, warps or ripples.
    • InstrumentsControls made of the fluid. They catch light, carry depth and recede while you read.

    Material behaviour: a shear-thickening fluid

    Like cornflour in water. At rest, surface tension keeps every shape round. The faster the force, the firmer the surface, so quick and repeated actions stay crisp while slow, exploratory ones get rich feedback; the shape gives, stretches and wobbles only while something acts on it, then rounds again. Every state below is the same fluid answering a different stress.
