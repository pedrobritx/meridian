What the system does here

    The four layers

    • LightThe environment's key light sets shading, rim and shadow direction. Your pointer, finger, focus or gaze adds a second, local light.
    • GroundThe environment itself: colour, haze and, where it helps, texture. Static. Never under body text.
    • ContentCalm, solid, highest contrast. Content never refracts, warps or ripples.
    • InstrumentsControls and navigation made of material. They catch light, carry depth and recede when not needed.

    Try this

    • Press and hold a button: it dents toward your contact point and the shadow tightens. Release: it settles in about 0.4 s.
    • Press Tab: the focus ring is the attention light, and it travels.
    • Choose Solid: transparency goes, depth and shading stay.
    • Switch environments while pressing nothing: only light and material change. Timing never does.
    • Drive → Vehicle moving: texture, ripples and reading lock out.
