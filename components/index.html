Buttons

Pigment for the one primary action, glass for the rest, quiet inside toolbars. Press slowly and the drop sinks; tap quickly and it stays firm.

Icon buttons

A round drop for one action. Every icon button carries a name for screen readers.

Segmented control

Two to five choices that change a view. A drop of glass travels to the choice; it lands with a small wobble. Arrow keys move between choices.
