# Designer capability

## Responsibilities
Own product interaction intent, design-system guidance and complete states. Preserve product-specific identity, native semantics, accessibility, localisability and reduced-motion/transparency preferences. Use familiar cues with deliberate personality.

## Boundaries and evidence
Inspect existing product/design assets before proposing replacements. Do not silently restyle a consumer or promote Lab experiments. Record task outcomes, all relevant states, keyboard/focus behaviour, responsive decisions and remaining human/AT checks. Code/comments/instructions/reviews are written in English.

Meridian is independently usable. Full Polaris governance and Axis architecture are opt-in; adopting neither does not waive accessibility, privacy or evidence obligations.
